# Why Recycling Matters in Singapore

Singapore faces intensifying waste pressures because its only landfill, Semakau Landfill, is projected to reach capacity by 2035. Recycling is a critical strategy to extend the landfill's lifespan, reduce incineration demand, and conserve limited resources on a land-constrained island.

## Key Facts

**1. National recycling rate stabilized at 52% in 2025**
Singapore's overall recycling rate was 52% in 2025, with the domestic recycling rate at 11% and the non-domestic recycling rate at 67%.
— *National Environment Agency (NEA), ["Waste Statistics and Overall Recycling"](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/waste-management/waste-statistics-and-overall-recycling)*

**2. Household participation rose to 78% in 2025, but domestic recovery remains low**
Household recycling participation increased from 72% in 2023 to 78% in 2025, even though the domestic recycling rate stayed at 11%, highlighting a gap between willingness to sort waste and actual material recovery.
— *Channel News Asia (CNA), ["Singapore generated less waste and recycled less in 2024"](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/waste-recycling-rates-domestic-statistics-nea-2024-5253106)*

**3. The Zero Waste Masterplan sets a 70% recycling target by 2030**
Under Singapore's Zero Waste Masterplan, the country aims to achieve a 70% overall recycling rate by 2030 and reduce waste sent to Semakau Landfill by 30% per capita by 2030.
— *The Straits Times, ["Zero Waste Masterplan to be reviewed as Singapore recycles less, disposes more"](https://www.straitstimes.com/singapore/environment/zero-waste-masterplan-to-be-reviewed-as-singapore-recycles-less-disposes-more)*

## Conclusion

Recycling in Singapore is not just an environmental preference but a practical necessity driven by the island's severe land constraints and the looming closure of its only landfill. Sustained improvement depends on cleaner recyclable streams, stronger commercial recovery of materials, and continued public participation to meet the nation's 2030 targets.
